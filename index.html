How it works

Three taps. Done.

No tutorials. No setup. Open the app and start tracking in under a minute.

1

Log the loan

Enter a name, an amount, and a quick note. Takes about 10 seconds — and your friend doesn't even need to be on the app yet.

2

They get notified

Your friend gets a ping and can confirm the transaction. Both sides see the same record — no more "I don't remember that."

3

Mark it settled

When money comes back — fully or in parts — mark it done. The history stays forever so there's never a dispute.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Five focused features. Each one there for a reason — to make sure you always know exactly where you stand with the people in your life.

🤝

Log a loan, tag a friend

Record who gave what to whom in seconds. Add a note so the reason is never forgotten — no matter how much time passes.

✅

Both sides confirm it

Your friend gets notified and confirms the transaction from their side. One shared record means no more "I don't remember that."

🔄

Settle in full or in parts

Getting paid back little by little? Log each partial payment as it comes. The remaining balance updates automatically.

Any currency, any country

Lending across borders or switching currencies mid-trip? Track every IOU in the currency it happened in.

See the full picture

Charts and reports show you exactly what you're owed, what you owe, and how your balances have moved over time.
