FAQ

Questions about tracking IOUs

Everything you need to know about keeping track of money you lend and borrow with IOU Money.

What is IOU Money?

IOU Money is a free IOU tracker app for iPhone and Android. It keeps a record of money you lend to friends and money you borrow, so you always know who owes what.

Is IOU Money free?

Yes. IOU Money is free to download and use on the App Store and Google Play.

Does my friend need the app too?

No. You can log a loan with someone who isn't on IOU Money yet. When they sign up, your past transactions with them sync to their account so both of you see the same record.

Can I track partial repayments?

Yes. If a friend pays you back a little at a time, log each partial payment and the remaining balance updates automatically.

Does it work with different currencies?

Yes. Every IOU can be recorded in the currency it happened in, which helps when you lend money while travelling or to friends abroad.

How is IOU Money different from a bill-splitting app?

Bill-splitting apps divide shared group expenses. IOU Money is built for personal loans: money you lend or borrow between two people, confirmed by both sides, with the full repayment history kept.
